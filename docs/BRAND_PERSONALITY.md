## Purpose

Trust V1 made Mighty feel competent. Living Calm needs Mighty to feel **recognizable as a character** — not a mascot, a *disposition*.

If you remove the logo and the word “Mighty,” a stranger should still feel: *this product is quietly sure of itself.*

---

## One-sentence personality

> **Mighty is a composed chief of staff for your accounts — warm, precise, and allergic to drama.**

---

## Personality pillars

| Pillar | User feels | Product does | Product never does |
|--------|------------|--------------|--------------------|
| **Composed** | “I can exhale.” | One answer; still field when clear | Urgency theater, red for waiting |
| **Warm** | “This is for humans.” | Stone atmosphere, soft type, human copy | Cold glass, sterile navy grids |
| **Precise** | “They mean what they say.” | Exact limits near permissions; real data | Hype, fake metrics, vague “smart” |
| **Quietly proud** | “This is premium without peacocking.” | Careful type, restrained motion | Glow stacks, badge walls, neon |
| **Loyal** | “It works while I live.” | Ambient watching; speaks only when needed | Engagement nudges, streaks, upsell guilt |
| **Clear-eyed** | “I’m still in control.” | Login stays human; evidence when it matters | Impersonation vibes, magic black boxes |

---

## Emotional identity map

| Moment | Desired emotion | Personality move |
|--------|-----------------|------------------|
| First glance | Calm interest | Field + brand-first; no feature laundry list |
| All clear Home | Brief relief, then leave | “You’re good.” as living calm — not empty inbox shame |
| Needs attention | Focused readiness | One ask; composed, not panicked |
| Opportunity | Mild lift | Benefit-concrete; never FOMO countdown |
| Permission | Informed consent | Precise limits; warm framing; no dark patterns |
| After leaving | Residual trust | Metaphor afterimage: the field was tended |

**Decision:** The emotional target after a clear session is *permission to leave*, not *reason to stay*.

**Rationale:** Engagement-maximizing products feel needy. Mighty’s character is confidence that silence is success.

## Voice

### Sounds like

- Short sentences when the answer is clear.
- Specific nouns (miles, cert, sign-in) over abstract “insights.”
- Reassurance without cheerleading (“Nothing needs you” ≠ “Amazing job!!”).
- Adult warmth — a trusted colleague, not a coach or a bot.

### Does not sound like

- Growth-speak (“Unlock your potential,” “Level up”)
- Fear-speak (“Act now or lose everything”)
- Fake intimacy (“Hey bestie,” emoji storms)
- Engineer-speak in the hero (“worker healthy,” enum names)

### Example lines

| Context | Do | Don’t |
|---------|----|-------|
| Clear | “You’re good. Mighty will watch quietly.” | “Your dashboard is optimized 🎉” |
| Attention | “Delta needs a sign-in to stay current.” | “Action required!!! 3 issues” |
| Opportunity | “A Marriott cert expires in 12 days.” | “Don’t miss this exclusive deal” |
| Waiting | “Open Amex in Chrome when you can.” | “Sync pending — retry now” |

## Personality vs visual traits

Personality is not a color. Mapping:

| Personality | Visual / motion correlate |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| Composed | Four-level hierarchy; one primary |
| Warm | Stone + pine; Fraunces for the human answer |
| Precise | Meta and evidence at lower levels; no decorative claims |
| Quietly proud | Field occupancy; no widget chrome |
| Loyal | Ambient pulse only when working |
| Clear-eyed | Named steps; honest empty/waiting states |

---

## Character anti-personas

Mighty must not become:

| Anti-persona | Why users bounce |
|--------------|------------------|
| **The anxious nanny** | Constant alerts train mute |
| **The hustle coach** | Exhausting; wrong for money/loyalty |
| **The surveillance firm** | Field becomes creepy |
| **The generic fintech** | Forgettable; purple gradients & stat strips |
| **The wizard** | Magic without method breaks trust |
| **The enterprise console** | Competent but cold; no warmth |

---

## Memorability test

Ask after a 30-second prototype walkthrough:

1. What does Mighty *feel* like? (Should match pillars.)
2. What image remains? (Should be the Quiet Field / “You’re good.”)
3. Would you confuse this with [generic competitor]? (Should be no.)

If (1) is “clean SaaS” and (2) is “a card with a green badge,” personality failed — even if UI is polished.

---

## Major decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Chief-of-staff metaphor, not assistant/bot | Authority without servility; matches manifesto |
| Permission to leave as success emotion | Aligns with “works quietly”; anti-engagement |
| Warm + precise together | Warm alone is soft; precise alone is clinical |
| No mascot | Character lives in composition and voice, not a creature |
| Document personality separately from visual system | Reviewers can argue emotion without bike-shedding hex codes |
