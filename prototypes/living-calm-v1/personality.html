Identity stage

Emotional identity

A composed chief of staff

Warm, precise, and allergic to drama. Mighty earns permission to leave — not permission to nag. Character lives in composition and voice, never a mascot.

Composed

One answer. Still field when clear. Urgency is rare and earned.

Warm

Human-scaled type and stone atmosphere — never cold glass tech.

Precise

Specific nouns. Honest limits. Real data or plain waiting — never hype.

Quietly proud

Premium without peacocking. Restraint is the flex.

Loyal

Works while you live. Speaks only when something is worth your time.

Clear-eyed

You stay the operator. Login is human. Evidence when claims matter.

Voice — do / don’t

Do

“You’re good. Mighty will watch quietly.”

“Delta needs a sign-in to stay current.”

“A Marriott cert expires in 12 days.”

Don’t

“Your dashboard is optimized 🎉”

“Action required!!! 3 issues”

“Don’t miss this exclusive deal”

Design decision — permission to leave

Engagement products optimize for dwell time. Mighty’s personality optimizes for residual trust after exit. If Home makes healthy users feel guilty for leaving, personality failed — even if the UI is polished. See docs/BRAND_PERSONALITY.md.
