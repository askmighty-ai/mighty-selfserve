Living Calm V1

Prototype-only exploration: Quiet Field as primary metaphor, a clear emotional identity, four hierarchy levels, and three alternative Home rituals. Does not replace Trust V1. Does not modify the production design system or customer UI.

Thesis

From polished interface to memorable product.

01 · Quiet Field V2

Metaphor, not motif

Decision: Promote Quiet Field from landing atmosphere to the product’s mental model. Accounts = steady points. Work = ambient pulse. Stillness = success. Attention = one rising signal.
Why: Memorable products own a metaphor. Wallpaper fields are forgotten.

One signal rises. The field stays calm.

02 · Brand personality

Composed chief of staff

Decision: Emotional identity = composed, warm, precise, quietly proud, loyal, clear-eyed. Success emotion after a clear session is permission to leave.
Why: Polish without character is forgettable; engagement-seeking character breaks trust.

Sounds like

“You’re good.”

Specific. Adult. No cheerleading.

Never becomes

Anxious nanny

Or hustle coach, wizard, enterprise console.

Afterimage

The tended field

Residual trust — not a reason to stay scrolling.

03 · Visual hierarchy

L1 Signal · L2 Story · L3 Evidence · L4 Chrome

Decision: Exactly four levels. One L1 per viewport. Evidence optional. Chrome never narrates.
Why: Named levels make visual competition a fail condition in review.

L1
You’re good.
L2
Mighty verified your accounts and will watch quietly.
L3
Amex · Delta · Marriott — verified this morning
L4
Working quietly · Last checked just now

04 · Home concepts · All clear

Three rituals, one question

Decision: Ship three competing Homes — not skins of one. Shared rule: answer “Does anything need me?” in five seconds.
Why: Premature convergence freezes the wrong daily ritual.

Minimal Calm

You’re good.

Glance → leave. No evidence. Field remnant only. Courage of emptiness.

Living Quiet Field

You’re good.

Full-bleed field. Points as presence. Highest metaphor memory.

Operational Calm

You’re good.

Field band + recent work + L4 ops strip. Proof without Control Tower.

06 · Home concepts · Opportunity

Benefit-concrete lift

Decision: Opportunity is a gentler lift — gold, not alarm — with concrete benefit copy. No FOMO countdowns, no secondary pitch walls.

Minimal Calm

One line. One action. Leave room.

Living Quiet Field

Soft gold lift. Still a field, not a deal page.

Operational Calm

Marriott cert · 12 days

07 · Review questions

What should we adopt?

Living Calm V1 does not pick a winner. Use this checklist in review:

  • Does Quiet Field feel like the product — or like marketing parked in the app?
  • Can you name Mighty’s personality in one sentence after thirty seconds?
  • Do the four hierarchy levels make competition obvious when it appears?
  • Which Home ritual would you open every morning — and which would you trust?
  • Is this more memorable than Trust V1 without getting louder?

Non-binding lean

Living Quiet Field for brand memory, tempered by Operational Calm’s L3 evidence rules in a future hybrid — but only after Minimal Calm is felt and consciously rejected or kept for clear-days.
