Connect Gmail to find your accounts

Mighty uses Gmail to discover loyalty and card accounts from your mail — not to manage your inbox.

  • Why we ask Program mail is the clearest signal of accounts you already have.
  • What is accessed Mail metadata needed for discovery — such as known program senders and related account signals — in a readonly posture.
  • What is not accessed Mighty does not send email as you, manage your inbox, or use Gmail as a general-purpose reader of unrelated personal mail.
  • What is stored Account candidates and the evidence summaries needed to show why they were found — so you can review and decide.
  • What happens next After you approve with Google, Mighty scans and shows what it found. You choose what to watch.
  • How to disconnect You can disconnect Gmail later from Mighty settings, and revoke access in your Google account.
