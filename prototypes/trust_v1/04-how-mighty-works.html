How Mighty works

Mighty finds loyalty and card accounts from your email, keeps them current while you browse in Chrome, and only asks you to sign in when necessary. You stay in control.

What Mighty does

  • Finds accounts from known program mail
  • Watches what you choose to keep
  • Speaks up only when something needs you

What you do

  • Connect Gmail once
  • Confirm what to watch
  • Sign into providers yourself when asked

What Mighty never does

  • Send email as you
  • Store provider passwords for discovery
  • Sign into providers as you

What happens next: After Gmail, Mighty scans for known programs and shows what it found for your review — nothing is watched until you confirm.
