How Mighty works

Mighty finds loyalty and card accounts from your email, keeps them current while you browse in Chrome, and only asks you to sign in when necessary. You stay in control.

What Mighty does

Watches accounts you already have and speaks up only when something needs you.

What you’ll do

Connect Gmail once. Later, sign into providers yourself when asked. Use desktop Chrome when verification needs the browser.

What happens next

After Gmail, Mighty scans for known programs and shows what it found for your review — nothing is watched until you confirm.

What we use Gmail for

  • Finding loyalty and card relationships from program mail
  • Matching known sender domains — not inventing accounts

What we will not do

  • Send email as you
  • Ask you to store provider passwords in Mighty for discovery
  • Sign into providers as you — you remain the operator
Connect Gmail Add an account manually instead

You can disconnect Gmail later from settings. Manual add is always available.
